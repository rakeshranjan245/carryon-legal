CarryOn

Privacy Policy & Aviation Safety Terms

Last updated: November 2026

1. What CarryOn is

CarryOn is a peer-to-peer marketplace that lets passengers on the same flight share spare baggage allowance. CarryOn is a neutral platform. It is not an airline, freight forwarder, courier or logistics provider, and it is not a party to any arrangement between users.

2. Mandatory open-box inspection

Every sender must agree, before submitting a request, to hand over all items fully unsealed at the airport. The carrier must physically inspect every item before airline check-in. Do not accept any item you have not personally inspected. If a person refuses inspection, walk away and report them.

3. Prohibited and restricted items

Users must never carry: loose lithium batteries or power banks in check-in baggage; unprescribed or controlled medicines; cash, jewellery or valuables; liquids, gels or powders over airline limits; weapons; and any illegal, hazardous or airline-restricted goods. You are solely responsible for the contents of any baggage you check in.

4. Your responsibility under airline and security rules

Airlines and aviation-security authorities hold the person who checks in a bag responsible for its contents. You must comply with all applicable airline policies and the laws of every country on your route. Only carry items for another person after a full open-box inspection and only if you are completely satisfied the items are legal and safe. If you are unsure, do not carry the item.

5. Privacy — what we collect and show

We store your email address for authentication only. It is never shown to other users. Public profiles display only your chosen display alias, email-verification badge, trip count and star rating. We never publish your phone number, full name or email. Phone numbers and emails typed in chat are automatically hidden to protect you. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

6. In-app chat

Chat only unlocks after a quote is accepted, and only between the two parties to that deal. Either user can tap "Walk Away / Cancel Deal" at any time before handover to immediately close the chat with zero obligation. Keep all communication in-app until you meet in person.

7. Safety, reporting and blocking

You can report or block any user from the chat screen. Reports are reviewed by our safety team and may lead to removal from the platform. We may suspend accounts that violate these terms. Never share personal contact details until you have met and are comfortable doing so.

8. No liability for user conduct

CarryOn does not verify the contents of any baggage and is not liable for loss, damage, delay, fines, confiscation or any consequence arising from arrangements between users. Use of the platform is at your own risk and subject to your own judgement.

9. Data deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will remove your profile, listings and messages within 30 days of a verified request.
